Accessibility basics

  • 1
    Always pair labels with inputs
    The label text should describe exactly what to enter.
  • 2
    Use fieldsets for related options
    Grouped radio buttons and checkboxes become easier to scan.
  • 3
    Keep focus visible
    Keyboard users need to see where they are on the page.
  • 4
    Make it responsive
    Stack columns and controls cleanly on small screens.
